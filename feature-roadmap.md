# Call-to-Predict feature roadmap

A browser app for talking to an AI assistant, exploring Gemini prediction markets and crypto order books, and approving sandbox prediction orders. Users review the preview in the dashboard and authorize the exact order over the phone.

## Workstreams

1. [Gemini Prediction Markets](docs/roadmap/01-gemini-prediction-markets/README.md) — Find a contract, approve its exact terms over the phone, and follow the order to its reported status.
2. [ElevenLabs Browser Voice](docs/roadmap/02-elevenlabs-browser-voice/README.md) — Use the browser for conversation and the dashboard to review a pending order before phone approval.
3. [Arc Research and x402](docs/roadmap/03-arc-research-x402/README.md) — Answer a question about a selected market and keep the evidence for any paid research.
4. [Crypto Order Book](docs/roadmap/04-crypto-order-book/README.md) — Explore Gemini spot order books for demo pairs such as BTC/USD and ETH/USD.
5. [New Dashboard](docs/roadmap/05-new-dashboard/README.md) — Connect the three product views and keep the voice session available while the user moves between them.
